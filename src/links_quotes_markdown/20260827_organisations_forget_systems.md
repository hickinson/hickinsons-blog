> Architecture as documentation captures the walls. Architecture as organisational memory captures the fires.

The wall/fire distinction is useful. We are often much better at documenting what exists than why somebody made the decision in the first place. AI makes recorded knowledge easier to interrogate, but it can't retrieve reasoning that walked out of the door years ago.
