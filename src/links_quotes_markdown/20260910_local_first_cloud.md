Cloud made infrastructure easier to acquire and, in some ways, harder to see. I'm interested in being able to reproduce important parts of a system locally too: testing without needing permission from somebody else's platform, keeping development isolated, and making the boundary between an application and its provider explicit.

Local-first isn't necessarily anti-cloud. Sometimes it is simply a way of keeping your options open.
