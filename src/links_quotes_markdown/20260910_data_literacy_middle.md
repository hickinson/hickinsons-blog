Top-down sponsorship matters. Grass-roots capability matters. There is still a large space between them where ambition has to become everyday behaviour. Middle layers translate priorities, allocate time, interpret rules and decide what people are actually allowed to do. Any literacy effort that misses that layer is missing part of the system.
