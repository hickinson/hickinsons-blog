Derbyshire isn't abstract to me. My grandparents were from Hathersage and I've traced my Hickinson line there back to the seventeenth century. Going down this rabbit hole was a reminder that the county which feels so fixed on a modern map wasn't fixed at all. [Mercia came and went](https://www.worldhistory.org/Kingdom_of_Mercia/), boundaries moved and identities changed. The family feels rooted there; the place itself has never stopped changing.
