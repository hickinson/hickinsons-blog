Detroit has always been my bag. I was deeply involved in dance music when I was younger, DJing and promoting, and I've never really lost the obsession with that late-eighties/early-nineties period: Chicago house, Detroit techno, the records travelling, and what happened when those sounds landed somewhere else. The UK turned that imported energy into its own Acid House explosion; Berlin took techno in another direction again. Delano Smith's mix feels connected to that lineage without sounding like a history lesson. I saved it because I still love the energy of the thing.
