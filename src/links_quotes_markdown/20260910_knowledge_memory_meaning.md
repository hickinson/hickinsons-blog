I've become increasingly interested in what an organisation actually remembers. Not what is stored, but what remains usable: definitions, decisions, context, exceptions and the reasons something became the way it is. AI makes retrieval easier, but that may not be the hardest problem. The harder question is what we choose to preserve, how we know where it came from and whether somebody ever judged it to be true.

This is a collection of things I'm exploring around organisational memory, knowledge, provenance and shared meaning.
